# Sound recordings

A sample of the clips I recorded on my phone while developing and testing SilentSignal (55 clips, about 5 minutes in total). Most of my testing was done live with real knocks and doorbells, so this is only part of what I tested with.

| Type | Files | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Knocks | `knock_01`–`knock_09`, `knock_loud`, `knock_soft`, `knock_noisy_1`–`2`, `knock_with_voices` | 14 |
| Doorbells | `doorbell_01`–`doorbell_12` | 12 |
| Background noise | `background_01`–`background_29` | 29 |

Format: `.m4a` (AAC), 48 kHz, recorded on a phone.
